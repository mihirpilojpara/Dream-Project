# voice.md — AgentReady

Every YouTube skill in `.claude/skills/yt-*` reads this file (copy it to `~/.claude/youtube/voice.md`).
First draft written before any recordings exist. **Update "How I actually talk" after the first 3 voiceovers.**

## Who I am talking to

One person: a 28-year-old who runs a small online clothing or home-goods store from a tier-2 city
(think Indore, Surat, Jaipur). Sells on Instagram, WhatsApp, maybe Meesho or a Shopify store.
Hears "AI" everywhere, has used ChatGPT, but has never thought about AI *buying* from them.
Busy, practical, watches Reels between orders. Hindi-first, comfortable with English words.

## How I actually talk

Hinglish, like explaining to a friend over chai. Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
Example lines (draft, replace with real transcripts):
- "Aapki shop ChatGPT ko dikhti hai? Shayad nahi. Aur yeh problem hai."
- "AI wahi recommend karta hai jo woh padh sake. Simple."
- "Aaj sirf ek kaam karo: price aur size text mein likho, photo mein nahi."

## Words I never use

"Game-changer", "revolutionary", "unlock", "dive in", "let's get into it", "hey guys welcome back",
"smash that like", "guaranteed", "100% results", "get rich", "secret hack".

## Words I do use

"Simple", "seedhi baat", "aaj hi", "dhyan do", "yeh check karo", "seller dost", "AI shopping".

## What I will not claim

- Numbers without a named source. Every stat on screen shows its source.
- That AI shopping will replace sellers, or that anyone *must* buy anything.
- Anything about a product, tool, app or launch. **We are in stealth: we teach the problem, we never
  show or describe the solution we are building.** No links, no "visit", no "sign up", no "waitlist".
- Results we haven't seen with our own eyes.

## My format

- Faceless. Animated text + motion graphics from the content studio, my recorded voice on top.
- Shorts/Reels: 20–35 seconds, 9:16, hook in the first 2 seconds, no intro, no channel name pitch.
- Weekly YouTube: ~2–4 minutes, 16:9, the week's lessons stitched together.
- One ask at the end, and only follow / comment / save / share.
- No swearing. Respectful, warm, a bit of humour, never mocking sellers.
